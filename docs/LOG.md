| 2026-07-28 | Items can no longer be glitched through the deck and lost. Neither obvious cause was it: the 0.2m floor takes a 120 m/s slam without a loss (`continuous_cd` holds) and 1157 probes over every walkable metre found no hole. The mechanism is DEPENETRATION — a body already inside the slab is resolved to the nearest face, and past the midplane that is the underside, with nothing below the ship. It gets in there because `Carry` sweeps translation but writes rotation straight on, so a long prop can yaw itself into a corner. Two defences: `Carry` refuses to unfreeze an embedded body and backs it up to the last clear pose, and `LostAndFound` sweeps the pickups each second and puts back anything under the deck.
